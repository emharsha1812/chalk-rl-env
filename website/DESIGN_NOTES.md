# Website design notes

The landing-page review draws on [Unslop](https://github.com/heydamir/unslop) by Damir Sokolovsky ([@heydamir](https://x.com/heydamir)), licensed under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

The local Manrope and Caveat fonts include their licenses in `content/assets/fonts/`.

The landing page draws on the [PanelUI web components](https://panelui.dev/docs/web/components) by Khalid Abdi. PanelUI is licensed under the MIT license; its notice is included in `content/assets/licenses/PanelUI-MIT.txt`. The designs are implemented in the site's existing HTML and CSS.

The landing page uses the supplied `Nocturne 064.png` image as its opening background. The cube diagram adapts the drawings and movement from the supplied `anatomy_of_an_rl_environment_interactive_cubic_room_dissection.html`, with simpler labels for Chalk's concepts.
